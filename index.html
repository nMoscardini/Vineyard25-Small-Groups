Vineyard25 Small Groups

Vineyard25 Small Groups is a free iPhone app with Bible studies for small groups. Each study has a short overview and a set of sessions to read, discuss, respond to and pray through, with notes for leaders kept separate.

Support

For questions, problems or suggestions, email admin@vineyard25.org.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-in, and the app works offline.

How do I find a study? Browse by Bible book, theme or title, or use search.

Can I hide the leader notes? Yes. Settings > Show leader notes in sessions.

How do new studies arrive? With app updates from the App Store.

Where is my data? On your iPhone only. See the privacy policy.
