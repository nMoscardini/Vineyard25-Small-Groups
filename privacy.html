Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Vineyard25 Small Groups is a free iPhone app with Bible studies for small groups and their leaders. It is published for Vineyard25, a church in Crowthorne, Berkshire, and built by Nino Moscardini.

The short version

Vineyard25 Small Groups does not collect any information about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

The app stores these things on your iPhone so it can work:

  • the library of studies, which comes with the app;
  • your bookmarks;
  • your place in each study and session;
  • the studies you opened recently;
  • your settings, such as text size, appearance and whether leader notes are shown.

None of this is sent to Vineyard25, to the developer or to anyone else. No one at Vineyard25 can see it.

No network use

The app does not connect to the internet. New studies and corrections arrive only with app updates from the App Store.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, your iPhone may include the app's data in that backup, as it does for most apps. That is handled by Apple and your own settings, not by the app.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Settings > Clear bookmarks and reading history removes your bookmarks and saved places. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting information, the policy will say so clearly before the app does.

Contact

Questions about privacy or the app: admin@vineyard25.org
